They say Scottish salmon is "gourmet dining".
It's been seasoned with lemon, dill and a cocktail of chemicals.

All Scottish salmon sold in UK supermarkets comes from factory farms, where flesh-eating sea lice are endemic. To treat parasitic infestations and other diseases, salmon are doused in toxic chemicals, including formaldehyde, deltamethrin and hydrogen peroxide.

Don't buy it.
