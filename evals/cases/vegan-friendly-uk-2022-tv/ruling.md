# ASA ruling: Vegan Friendly UK, June 2022
Outcome: Upheld in part (2 of 3 issues: distress and scheduling upheld; offence to meat eaters not upheld)
Medium: TV (seen March 2022; Clearcast restriction: not in or near programmes for under-16s)
Claim type: imagery
CAP clauses: BCAP (broadcast code). Breached: 1.2, 4.1, 4.10, 32.1, 32.3. Investigated, no breach: 4.2, 4.8.
What the ad said: Three adults eat and chat about social and environmental issues, intercut with quick clips of animals and meat and fish preparation. Ends with "MAKE THE CONNECTION".
Why: Fast editing, close focus on the animals and the contrast with people eating made some clips likely to distress, and one was judged gratuitous. The ASA said this content was unsuitable for TV whatever the scheduling. The portrayal of meat eaters was exaggerated but used no derogatory language, so it did not cause widespread offence by vilifying them.
Lesson: Highlighting meat eaters' inconsistency is acceptable; fast-cut slaughter footage on TV is not, even with scheduling restrictions.
