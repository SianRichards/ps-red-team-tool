# ASA ruling: Viva!, May 2018
Outcome: Upheld
Medium: bus posters (seen September 2017)
Claim type: statistic / health
CAP clauses: Breached: 3.1, 3.7 (CAP Code Edition 12)
What the ad said: Image of a cow's udder with "dairy industry facts", including that milk contains 35 hormones and that "some of these are linked to cancer".
Why: The ASA said consumers would read "linked to" as meaning drinking milk could raise cancer risk, even though the ad didn't say "causes". Viva! had evidence for the 35 hormones but not for the cancer link, so the claim was misleading. Viva! was told not to state or imply that hormones in milk increase cancer risk.
Lesson: Softer wording ("linked to") doesn't help if the overall impression still makes a claim you can't prove.
Link: https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/viva--a17-398588.html
