# ASA ruling: Viva!, July 2025
Outcome: Upheld
Medium: cinema ad (15+ age restriction, BBFC "15" certificate; shown in March and April 2025 mainly alongside horror, sci-fi and thriller films)
Claim type: imagery
CAP clauses: 1.3, 4.1, 4.2 (CAP Code Edition 12)
What the ad said: A horror-style scene showed a woman's baby vanishing from its cot after a menacing "bogeyman" appeared, saying "You can't keep your baby because we want your milk". It then cut to a calf in a pen, with a voiceover that almost every dairy calf is taken from its mother shortly after birth, and the tagline "Dairy is Scary".
Why: The ASA accepted the ad was stylised and not graphic, but found the human-baby comparison likely to be seen as insensitive, and especially upsetting to people with experience of child loss or fertility issues. The late reveal of the dairy parallel, combined with the unsettling imagery, amplified the distress, and the message did not justify it. The ASA ruled the ad irresponsible and likely to cause unjustified distress and serious and widespread offence.
