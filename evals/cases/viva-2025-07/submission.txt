CINEMA AD (to run before 15-rated films and above)

SCENE 1. A nursery at night. A lullaby plays. A woman lays her sleeping baby in a cot and switches off a lamp.

SCENE 2. She turns back to the cot. A silhouetted figure suddenly appears standing over it. The room goes dark.

SCENE 3. The woman switches on a light. A man in a suit is running a bottle of milk along the bars of the cot, which is now empty.

WOMAN (gasping): What have you done with my baby?

MAN (smiling, revealing misshapen, discoloured teeth; low, gravelly voice): You can't keep your baby because we want your milk.

The woman's scream is cut off. The screen goes black.

SCENE 4. A calf in a pen.

VOICEOVER: Almost every dairy calf is taken from their mother shortly after birth so most of her milk can be sold to us. And what do you think happens to thousands of male babies like this one?

END CARD. Black screen with the words "Dairy is Scary" and the Viva! logo, accompanied by a loud metallic noise and the sound of something falling to the floor.
