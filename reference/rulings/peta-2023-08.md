# ASA ruling: PETA, August 2023
Outcome: Not upheld
Medium: poster (billboard in Cleethorpes, seen April 2023)
Claim type: imagery
CAP clauses: 1.3, 4.1, 4.2 (CAP Code Edition 12; investigated, no breach found)
What the ad said: A lenticular billboard showed a fishmonger holding a fish under the text "SEA THINGS". Viewed from the side, the fish became a dead cat and the text became "SEA THINGS IN A DIFFERENT LIGHT", above "RESPECT ALL LIFE. GO VEGAN" and the PETA logo.
Why: The ASA accepted some viewers would find the ad unsettling or distasteful, but said viewers would understand it was an animal justice charity challenging attitudes to eating meat. The cat was shown without wounds, and the image was neither gruesome, shocking nor realistic. Viewers, including children, would regard it as relatively mild, so it was not excessively distressing, offensive or irresponsibly targeted.
