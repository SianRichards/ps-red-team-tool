# ASA ruling: RSPCA, May 2025
Outcome: Not upheld
Medium: YouTube video, TV, poster (seen May 2024)
Claim type: "humane" / welfare standards
CAP clauses: Investigated, no breach: CAP 3.1, 3.3; BCAP 3.1, 3.2.
What the ad said: Animals in harmful situations, including on farms, followed by animals being cared for, with the message that every kind of animal deserves kindness. Complainants said it misrepresented welfare on RSPCA Assured farms.
Why: Viewers would expect RSPCA standards to mean high farm welfare. The RSPCA Assured standards go beyond the legal minimum (e.g. no farrowing crates, more space and enrichment), and the ASA saw no evidence that non-compliance was widespread: an independent 2024 audit found 93% of sampled farms had fewer than five non-compliances.
Lesson: Welfare claims stand if backed by documented standards and audit data. Campaigners challenging "higher welfare" claims need evidence of widespread problems, not isolated cases.
