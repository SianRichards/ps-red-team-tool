# ASA ruling: Viva!, September 2023
Outcome: Upheld (both issues)
Medium: social (paid video ads on Facebook Watch and Instagram, plus in-app ads on Duolingo and Poki Games, seen May 2023)
Claim type: imagery
CAP clauses: 1.3, 4.1, 4.2 (CAP Code Edition 12)
What the ad said: A parody "Killer Yoghurt" ad showed a smiling woman eating yoghurt filled with bloody offal, with blood dripping from her mouth. The voiceover said the yoghurt was made from "the stolen milk of grieving mothers" and urged viewers to "Taste the torment" and "Be complicit". It then cut to an indoor dairy shed.
Why: The ASA accepted the parody and the animal welfare message, but found the graphic, gory close-ups, set against the woman's cheerful demeanour, likely to shock and disgust. It found the language, such as "umbilical cord flavour", likely to frighten and distress children in particular, so the distress was unjustified. Viva!'s targeting exclusions (over-18s, chosen interests) were insufficient because the ads reached children via Duolingo and Poki Games through the Google display network.
